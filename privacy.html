Privacy Policy

Home Assistant Nest is a personal, non-commercial app used only by its owner.

What the app accesses

The app accesses only the owner's own Google Nest thermostat data through Google's Smart Device Management API. This is used solely to display and control the thermostat in the owner's own Home Assistant installation.

What is collected, stored and shared

The app does not collect, sell or share any data with third parties. Data from the thermostat stays within the owner's own Home Assistant installation and Google Cloud project.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from their Google Account's third-party access settings.
